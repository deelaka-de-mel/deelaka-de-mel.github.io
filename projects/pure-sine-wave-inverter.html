Pure Sine Wave Inverter

A 300 W pure sine wave inverter operating at 12 V, designed around a push-pull converter and transformer stage that steps voltage to a high-voltage AC bus.

Hardware · Power Electronics · Completed

PCB

Gallery

Overview

I was in charge of designing the Converter stage to step up 12V to 400V.

Motivation

To build an efficient push pull converter that could maintain a constant 400V in spite of load variations.

Problem Statement

The primary challenge was creating a reliable high-voltage conversion stage while preserving a well-controlled output waveform and manageable hardware complexity.

Features

  • Push-pull converter stage
  • Custom wound Transformer-based voltage step-up

Hardware Used

  • SG3525
  • EE55 transformer core
  • IR2110 gate drivers

Software Used

  • LTspice
  • Altium Designer

Challenges Faced

  • Transformer design and winding
  • Maintaining a constant output voltage under varying load conditions
  • Ensuring accurate and stable operation of the push-pull converter stage

    • Future Improvements

    Improving efficiency, thermal performance, and integration for a more production-ready power conversion board.
